Langar Seva

Guru Nanak Darbar Gurudwara, Jebel Ali opened in 2012 as the UAE's first official gurudwara. Its langar is a free community kitchen open to every faith. Seva means selfless service.

  • Organised the food line and helped set things up
  • Served food to everyone who came
  • Then sat in pangat and ate langar ourselves

“Pehle pangat, phir sangat.”

First sit and eat together as equals, then pray together. (Guru Amar Das)

On the fourteenth of November we went to Guru Nanak Darbar Gurudwara in Jebel Ali, the UAE's first official gurudwara. Its langar, the community kitchen, feeds anyone of any faith for free. We helped organise the dal, roti, chawal and meethe chawal, and served it to everyone. Then we sat down and ate langar ourselves, side by side with strangers.

The next morning, at 5 a.m., we were at Dubai Design District for the Emirates NBD Unity Run, organised by FittGROUP. It's a run built around including People of Determination. In our volunteer jackets we guided runners to the start line, handed out around eighty UAE flags between us, gave out water at the finish, and put a medal around every finisher's neck.
How did it impact the community?

What it gave others

Up to 3 patients helped by one donation

Each unit splits into red cells, plasma and platelets. We gave 2 units and helped every donor we guided feel at ease.

A free hot meal with dignity, for anyone

No one is asked who they are. Everyone sits at the same level and eats the same food.

Inclusion you can see on the street

Runners of every age and ability, with People of Determination running for free with a companion.

Research says

Voluntary, unpaid donors are the safest blood supply. They gave over 85% of the world's ~120 million donations.

WHO Global Database on Blood Safety, 2023 data
Research says

People who eat with others more often feel happier, more trusting and more engaged with their local community.

Dunbar (2017), Breaking Bread, Adaptive Human Behavior & Physiology
Research says

Across 515 studies, contact between groups reliably reduces prejudice. Shared events like this run are that contact.

Pettigrew & Tropp (2006), meta-analysis, J. Personality & Social Psychology

passing the baton → Vedansh

So what did this give the community? One blood donation can help up to three patients, and voluntary donors like us are the world's safest blood supply. Langar gives anyone a free meal with dignity, and research shows that eating together builds trust and belonging. At the run, contact between people of different abilities is one of the most reliable ways prejudice falls. Vedansh will now tell you what it did for us.

Alignment to the happiness strategies

Every stop pulls many threads

Now the heart of it: the happiness strategies. Every activity was an act of kindness, and we did every one together, which is nurturing social relationships. Seva at the gurudwara is gratitude in practice. The run and the langar were care for body and soul. And handing water to a runner keeps you completely in the present. Donating blood meant managing our own nerves first.
Additional research on the strategies

Why it made us happier

Blood camp · kindness

Blood donors are driven by “warm glow”, the good feeling of giving itself, more than by pure concern for patients.

Ferguson et al. (2012), Health Psychology
Langar · relationships

Very happy people share one trait above all: strong social relationships. Langar is built on sitting together.

Diener & Seligman (2002), Psychological Science
Unity Run · body & present

Volunteers at community running events report a stronger sense of community and better well-being.

Stevinson et al. (2015), parkrun study
The thread tying all three together

Doing 5 kind acts in one day raised happiness more than spreading them over a week. Our blood-camp day and our seva-then-run weekend were exactly that kind of burst.

Lyubomirsky, Sheldon & Schkade (2005), Review of General Psychology

Volunteering raises happiness, life satisfaction, self-esteem and sense of control, and happier people volunteer more. It's a loop.

Thoits & Hewitt (2001), J. Health & Social Behavior

Spending on others, not ourselves, makes people happier, whether the gift is money, blood, food or time.

Dunn, Aknin & Norton (2008), Science

The research backs this up for each stop. Blood donors are driven by "warm glow", the good feeling of giving itself. Very happy people share one trait: strong social relationships, which is exactly what langar is built on. And volunteers at community runs report a stronger sense of community. Tying all three together: Lyubomirsky found that five kind acts in a single day lift happiness more than spreading them out, and that is how our weekend worked. Thoits and Hewitt showed volunteering raises happiness and self-esteem.
Contradictions · re-examined positions

Where class and reality disagreed

In classHelping only lifts well-being when it's freely chosen, not done for a reward.Weinstein & Ryan, 2010
We foundWe started for a grade, yet it felt real. Somewhere between the forms and the medals, our motivation became our own.
In classAbout 40% of happiness is under our intentional control, the "happiness pie".Lyubomirsky et al., 2005
We foundWe could only help because we were healthy enough to donate, free on a weekend, and in a city that makes volunteering easy. Circumstances mattered more than the pie implies.cf. Brown & Rohrer, 2020
In classAn act of kindness means a giver helps a receiver.
We re-examinedAt langar we served, and then the same people served us. Kindness felt less like charity and more like belonging.
position re-examined
We also found contradictions. Weinstein and Ryan show helping only lifts well-being when it's freely chosen, and honestly, we started for a grade. But somewhere between the forms and the medals it stopped being about marks. Second, the "40 percent" happiness pie: we could only help because we were healthy, free and in a city that makes volunteering easy, so circumstances matter more than the pie suggests. And at langar we stopped seeing kindness as charity. There's no giver and receiver, just community.
